Uniform if the NB interval is honest. Pile-up at the ends means the band is too tight.

Limitations

No ICU step-down, no theatre list, no ambulance offload. Coverage is the honesty check; MAE is the scoreboard. If coverage collapses, the twin is no longer a product.
