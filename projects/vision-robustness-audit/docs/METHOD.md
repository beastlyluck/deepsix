# Method — Vision robustness

15 corruptions × 3 severities × 4 backbones. CE = backbone error / reference error × 100. mCE is the mean. Traffic light on accuracy drop vs clean: green ≤10pt, amber ≤25, red after.

First red severity per corruption becomes a sentence: “Do not update inventory under rain severity ≥ 3.” That sentence is the slash.
