ForgeX notes

Late-fusion cell twin. Acoustics, current and a camera are scored separately, then combined. The interlock is not part of the model.

Late-fusion pipeline

1. Cycle ends. Three feature blocks land: 9 acoustic, 4 current, 9 vision.

2. IsolationForest on the stack — unsupervised trip for the desk.

3. Logistic on the same stack — published score, with leave-one-block-out AUC.

4. Residual from the friction/thermal model is a second input to the FSM, not a fourth feature. Mixing it into the logit hides a cooling-duct fault as “just another score.”

If acoustic AUC stays high and vision AUC collapses when leaks are removed from the label set, the camera is doing its job and only its job.

Raw acoustic to bands

x, 16 kHz, 0.25 s
stft nperseg=256, overlap=128
band means: 80–160, 160–320, 320–400, 400–440,
            440–520, 520–800, 800–1600, 1600–4000 Hz
+ crest factor

The 400–440 Hz bin is the mesh. Wear lives in the neighbouring bins. We do not ship a spectrogram image model to the drive cabinet.

Override flowchart

score < 0.35 and residual < 4 K     → NORMAL
score ≥ 0.35 or residual ≥ 4 K      → WATCH  (still moving)
score ≥ 0.65 or residual ≥ 8 K      → HOLD   (motion gated)
stall flag OR two HOLDs in 5 cycles → ESTOP
HOLD / ESTOP leave only on reset=1

A score that dips after HOLD is not a reset. That path is how a cracked reducer is run for another hour.

Edge artefact

outputs/weights.json holds mean, scale, coef, intercept. edge.actuator.score is one dot product. Latency is printed in microseconds on each run.
